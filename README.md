# Minecraft-Mod-Releases
Downloads for Gublun’s Minecraft Forge 1.7.10 mods.

## Botany-Extensions 1.0.0

Mana automation for Botania and Applied Energistics 2.

- [Download Botany-Extensions 1.0.0](./botanyextensions-1.0.0.jar?raw=true)
- Minecraft 1.7.10, Forge 10.13.4.1614, Botania r1.8-249, Applied Energistics 2 rv3-beta-6, and Baubles 1.0.1.10.

Place the JAR in your modpack's `mods` folder.

## SoulCC 1.0.6

- [Download SoulCC 1.0.6](./SoulCC-1.0.6.jar?raw=true)
- Minecraft 1.7.10 and Forge 10.13.4.1614. Requires IndustrialCraft 2 and Extra Utilities. BuildCraft Silicon enables the three laser tiers.
- Updated textures and interfaces, a new tablet shop assortment, tiered laser recipes, and solar egg rewards from IC2, Advanced Solar Panels, and Industrial Upgrade when present.
- Each incubator egg costs 100 coins in the shop. The solar egg no longer awards the three original SoulCC panels.

Replace the previous SoulCC JAR in your modpack's `mods` folder. This is the exact build tested in the modpack (`d8f3029`). Its internal build label is `890747e-pack-test`.
